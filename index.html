About the Game

Roar & Rescue: Tigers vs Goats is a strategy board game where three tigers face fifteen goats in a battle of movement, capture and strategy.

Features

🐯 Play as Tigers or Goats

🤖 Play against AI opponents

🎮 Three AI difficulty levels

👥 Local two-player gameplay

🧠 Simple rules with strategic gameplay
